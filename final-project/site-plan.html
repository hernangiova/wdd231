Explore Guatemala

Site Plan

Site Name

Explore Guatemala

The name "Explore Guatemala" was selected because it represents the purpose of the website. It will help visitors discover interesting and beautiful places throughout Guatemala.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Guatemala is to provide information about different tourist destinations in Guatemala. Visitors will be able to see images, descriptions, and information about different places they can visit.

Scenarios

  • What are some interesting places I can visit in Guatemala?
  • What does a tourist destination in Guatemala look like?
  • What activities can I do at a specific destination?

Color Scheme

The website will use simple colors inspired by the sky and nature of Guatemala.

  • Sky Blue (#87CEEB): Header, footer, and buttons.
  • Black (#000000): Text and navigation.
  • White (#FFFFFF): Main background.

Typography

The website will use Lucida Sans for headings, navigation, paragraphs, and general content. This font is simple and easy to read.

Content

The home page will include a hero section introducing Guatemala and a Featured Places section.

Each destination will have an image, the name of the place, a short description, and a Learn More link.

Possible Places

  • Antigua Guatemala
  • Lake Atitlán
  • Tikal
  • Semuc Champey
  • Acatenango Volcano
  • Rio Dulce
